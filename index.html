Featured Applications

CHO-CHA-DA SME Assistant (v1.0)

An offline-first Point of Sale (POS) and inventory companion tailored for retail booths, street food stalls, and bakeries. It enables vendors to execute error-free checkouts and stock updates within 5 seconds.

Key Subsystems:
  • Scan & Sell Terminal: Instant QR-code order building with a built-in double-scan prevention slider (1-5 seconds).
  • Local Inventory Tracker: Smart low-stock visual alerts and raw materials auditing.
  • Secure Ecosystem: Enterprise-grade 6-digit PIN and biometric fingerprint verification.
  • Data Independence: 100% offline database storage with Excel-compatible CSV data backup.
