About the Developer

MaeChada (แม่ชฎา), founded by Chadchadaporn Pukkaew, is an independent mobile application developer and data analyst specialising in offline-first tools for micro-entrepreneurs and small businesses. With a background in Information Systems and Artificial Intelligence, MaeChada engineers practical solutions guided by a "Zero-Learning Curve" design philosophy.

Our target users — coffee shops, street-food vendors, weekend market stalls, and home-based entrepreneurs — deserve the same powerful tools as large enterprises, built for real-world conditions with limited connectivity and varying levels of digital literacy.

Privacy & Compliance

All applications published under the MaeChada brand are built with privacy-by-design principles and comply with Google Play Developer Programme Policies.

  • ✓ All user data stored locally on-device — no external transmission (Standalone version)
  • ✓ No personal information collected: no name, email, phone, or GPS location
  • ✓ PIN stored as SHA-256 hash + salt — the actual PIN is never readable by anyone
  • ✓ Camera used exclusively for QR code scanning — no photos captured or stored
  • ✓ No third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or data brokers
  • ✓ Users can export, clear, or permanently delete all data at any time
  • ✓ Not directed at children under 13 years of age (COPPA compliant)
